FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. No account, no uploads.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use Settings → Export my data; device/iCloud backups also carry your data.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Giydim. Access continues until the period ends.

My purchase isn't showing.

Giydim → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Weather isn't showing.

Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Giydim → "While Using the App".

How many items can I add for free?

30 items. Unlimited with Pro.
