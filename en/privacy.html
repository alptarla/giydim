Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026

Summary

  • Giydim has no accounts. Your wardrobe, outfits and calendar are stored only on your device.
  • Photos and background removal (Apple Vision) are processed on device and never uploaded.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Data we handle

Wardrobe data

Items, photos, outfits and wear history live in the app's on-device database and never reach us. If device backups are enabled they may be included in your iCloud/device backup, governed by Apple's policies.

Camera and photos

Used only when you add an item and grant permission. Processing happens on your device.

Location (weather)

If you allow it, your approximate location is sent to Apple WeatherKit to fetch today's weather. We do not store or share your location. If you don't allow location, you can pick a city instead; it is stored only on your device, the search goes to Apple Maps and the city's location to Apple WeatherKit. You can turn weather off entirely in Settings.

Purchases

Giydim Pro is sold through the Apple App Store. Subscription status is verified with RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives only a randomly generated anonymous app user ID and purchase/subscription details. Your name, email and payment details never reach us or RevenueCat; Apple processes payment.

Export and deletion

Settings → Export my data gives you everything as JSON and PNG; Delete all data permanently removes it from the device. Deleting the app also removes it.

Children

Giydim is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
